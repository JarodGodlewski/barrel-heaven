## Cite → Nest-clothes

### Vampire Survivors (2021) — verb, then a body

**Cite.** Luca Galante / poncle. Character door is a stat sheet plus a starting weapon (Noclip). Unlock menu is a verb: Fire Wand to 4, Garlic to 7, recover 1,000 HP, survive 20:00 with Curse, open a coffin, kill 3,000 of one thing (Polygon 2022; vampire.survivors.wiki). Antonio is free. After the verb, gold still buys the body; cost climbs with roster size. After the first extra unlock, silhouettes of the rest appear. Secret characters had no node — they needed a guide. Roster grew until "just stats" was not enough and each hull wanted a unique rule.

**Steal.** Verb you already do. Rook = Antonio (default). Spare crate = second body after you already survived the job. Banner dart = keep-flying body (3 clears) with one secret key (Kite). Silhouettes, not empty holes.

**Reject.** 20+ hulls. Pause Unlocks encyclopedia. Gold-to-buy the hull after the verb. Secret spell codes. A unique starting gun per kit (KITS.md: Twin Laser on all three). If a fourth hull wants a unique *rule*, that is a Heresy, not a kit.

### Binding of Isaac: Rebirth (2014) — marks, not a wiki

**Cite.** Edmund McMillen, Gamasutra 2012. Beating Mom is a fake ending. Polaroid / Negative persist; the movie is a recipe *that run*. Completion marks: a tiny post-it on the character door (Rebirth wiki; Arqade 2014). Black fill = you did it. Red outline = Hard. You do not start in the Chest. Polaroid started too secret; he made the drop obvious and kept the destination obscure.

**Steal.** Three kits × five jobs on the kit door. Unlock ≠ skip. Lock is never a launch button (you do not start in the Chest; Star Fox does not start at Area 6). Medal *is* the photo — no Polaroid trinket (CLASSICS).

**Reject.** 24 marks. Tainted twins. Flavour text per stamp. A sixth column.

### Star Fox 64 (1997) — verb in the flight

**Cite.** Hidden routes are in-flight verbs (save Falco, seven arches, Macbeth switches). Radio names the fork, then shuts up. All medals unlock Expert; they do not by themselves open Venom 2 (Iwata Asks; CLASSICS).

**Steal.** Verb-in-the-flight lights a node. Radio, one line. Miss a medal, fall back to A (Katina, not Venom).

**Reject.** Hit-count medals as the B gate. Expert as a sixth job. Lylat sheet on the door.

### Super Mario 64 (1996) — star = a verb in the room

**Cite.** Miyamoto (shmuplations): star = ride the owl, shoot the cannon, drown in the moat. Castle doors are count gates, not chapters. He did not want to show where the Star appears.

**Steal.** Medal, hop-pack, buoy, stay, Kite-hears-you are stars. Marks are those doors, Isaac-sized.

**Reject.** Castle hub. Paintings that are other planets. HUD arrow.

### Spelunky HD (2012) — unlock in the room

**Cite.** Four bodies free, sixteen more in coffins in the level. Differences cosmetic. HD added clues when mute was too mute (Yu / Eurogamer).

**Steal.** Unlock in the flight, shareable ("hop eight"). Pip's one line is the Udjat ping.

**Reject.** Sixteen skins. Kits are biases on eight printed stats, not hats.

### Rogue Squadron (1998) — medal unlocks extras, badly

**Cite.** Gold on every primary unlocks a bonus *level*. Medal is five bars. Passcodes exist because the curve failed (`IGIVEUP`).

**Steal.** Clean flight proves you can ride the peel.

**Reject.** Bonus planets. Results-screen essay. Extras are Dry Mouth and inverse curtain — same hole.

## Jobs (nodes on the door, not a map)

Five attitudes toward one hole. Verb *once* lights the node forever. Ending B still wants the string **that night**. Forks in `SECRETS.md`. This table is only what the door remembers.

| Job | Verb that lights it | Hangar later | Mark when |
|-----|---------------------|--------------|-----------|
| **The Well** | default. Always first. | always | survive 10:00 (A) |
| **Scrap Wake** | **medal** = never drop hull | not a free encore — peel will not take a sloppy hull | you rode the peel |
| **Dry Mouth** | scoop the **off-cardinal buoy** (Isaac unlock). **Stay** 30s after Mercy warps *tonight* | next launch offers it | you looted the empty mouth (C) |
| **Gold Paint** | **Kite hears you**, *or* arrive via the true string | yes as the *wrong window*; B still goes through it that night | you dumped out the gold lip |
| **the Lock** | true string that night: Well (medal) → Wake → don't chase Mercy → Paint → in | **never a launch button** | you came out the other side (B) |

Hop-pack (hop 8+ in one go) is how you *execute* Wake, not a sixth node. No-bomb True is a Lock rule that night, not a door row. Never-Q is not the Well medal (`SECRETS.md`).

Radio, one line, then shut up — copy is not this door: Pip "Wake's open." / Hatch "She's gone. You're just greedy." / Kite "Wrong window, Rook." / Hatch on the Lock says almost nothing.
