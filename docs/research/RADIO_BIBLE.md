# Radio bible

Three radios. Crew channel plus one gift-shop leak. Rook is silence.
`docs/WORLD.md` is law. This file is how they talk. Not a script dump. Not a map.

Pip names the fork, one line, then shut up. Hatch almost says the Well thing,
then tells you to roll. Kite is 1–2 times. If a line sounds like a tweet, a
briefing, a patch-notes apology, or a trailer, cut it.

**Off comms this cart:** Vicar. Juno. Bogons. A Guide. Anyone else.

Sibling `ENDINGS.md` owns what we SEE and how long. This file owns who is on
the horn. Do not invent a fourth voice to fill silence.

## Hatch

- Mild bite: mad, boring, fly smart.
- Calling the job a job. Hold the mouth. Ten minutes.

- Trailer paste: “this is our hole,” “we are Nest.”

He almost says the Well thing. Then he tells you to roll. That swallow is
the only philosophy he is allowed.
