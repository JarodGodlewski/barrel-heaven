## Cite

**Vampire Survivors (2021), character door.** Luca Galante / poncle. Noclip: early characters were a *stat sheet plus a starting weapon*; unique rules got bolted on later because "just stats" went ridiculous as the roster grew. Patch 0.2.8 (2022-01-16, Steam news): after a couple of unlocks, a stats panel appears on character select; pause shows current stats. Wiki (vampire.survivors.wiki): white = kit base / Golden Eggs, yellow = PowerUps. Antonio (default): Max Health 120, Armor +1, every other printed stat "-". Clerici: Max Health 150, Recovery 0.50, Area +400% that dies 100% per level until 5. Dommario: MoveSpeed -40%, Duration +40%, projectile Speed +40%. Christine: Max Health 50, MoveSpeed +30%, Might -35%, Cooldown -25%. Krochi: MoveSpeed +30%. Porta: Area +30% and Cooldown -90% that climbs back — a *rule*, not a bias. Golden Egg = +1 to one stat on that character (1% on a % stat, +1 HP, +1 Amount).

**What they did.** Runs break because the same short list multiplies. The door *is* the build. Starter is almost even. Tank is HP/Area. Glass is HP down, fire-rate and feet up. Slow brick is MoveSpeed down. Amount and Cooldown are the run-breakers; they are stingy on the door (Gennaro's +1 Amount is the exception; Porta's -90% Cooldown is a gimmick).

**F-Zero X/GX.** Imamura / Sakamoto (shmuplations 2003): machine select is a graph (top speed vs accel), not a biography. Speed is a lie of frame rate. CLASSICS.md already: kit select as a graph is legal; kit select as a lore page is not.

**Isaac completion marks.** McMillen, Gamasutra 2012; Road to v1.0 Day 5 (Steam, 2022-10-11): a tiny per-character stage grid, disable-able. Recipe lives in the flight, not in a paragraph under the sprite.

**Crimsonland (2003).** Survival starts 100 HP, pistol, no perks. Loadouts are perk packages, almost no story. CLASSICS.md already: Banner dart / Spare crate / Rook's hull are Crimsonland loadouts with Nest names. Reject perk-every-level if it pauses more than the 3-card.
