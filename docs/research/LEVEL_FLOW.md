## Steal two structures. Reject the wiki.

### Star Fox 64 (1997) — radio is the map, the string is that night

**Cite.** Nintendo EAD. Route sheet: 16 missions, 25 paths, 14 to Venom (fake Andross), 11 to Venom 2 (real). Hidden routes are in-flight verbs (save Falco, seven arches, Macbeth switches, Sector Z missiles). Radio tells you the fork, then shuts up. Medals are a hit-count second game; they do not by themselves open Venom 2. Official JP guidebook (Imamura / Miyamoto / Shimizu) and Iwata Asks: Star Fox 64 3D — drama is teammates dropping out mid-flight, not a briefing essay. They needed Nintendo Power for the warps. That is the cost we refuse.

**Did.** Easy path is the first clear. Hard path is a string you execute *that night*. Fake final on the easy approach. Warp gates are geometry you fly, not a pause menu.

**Nest-clothes.** Steal the *string*, not the 16 planets. The Well is always first (Corneria). Survive = Bolse → Venom 1 = ending A (radio, hangar, fake bow). Miss the medal, fall back to A — Katina instead of Venom. Medal + hop-pack = the hard fork; Pip one line, then shut up. Scrap Wake → Gold Paint → the Lock = Area 6 → Venom 2, **one sitting**. Fake Andross = A's hangar. Real Andross = the Lock (Q refused). Unlock ≠ skip (Isaac clothes, already in PROGRESSION).

**Reject.** Pause-map with 16 names. Route diagram on the kit door (tiny Isaac marks only). Medals-as-hit-count as the *key* to B. Strategy-guide warps. "True ending unlocked" toast. Hatch explaining Katina.

### OutRun (1986) — the fork is in the world

**Cite.** Yu Suzuki / Sega AM2. Retro Gamer #54; Eurogamer "Out Ran." Not a racing game, a *driving* game. Forks at every checkpoint; left generally easier, right harder; five goals, fifteen roads. The route map *after* the run shows you only saw a slice. WIRED (2021): a game about choice, and showing the route at the end was ingenious. First stage is almost empty of traffic so the new player is not punished for existing.

**Did.** You commit with the car, not a menu. Difficulty is the road you can see. The clock is the enemy.

**Nest-clothes.** Steal: forks you can *see*. When the medal is live, Scrap Wake is weather in the sky — peel, junk halo — not a pause unlock. Gold Paint as the "wrong window" is the tourist road: you can see the gold lip, Kite is the billboard, you still might take it. Dry Mouth is the road that stays in the empty mouth after the easy goal. Pip still names the hard fork because one-finger players may not look up. Radio is backup for thumbs. If only radio, we threw away the OutRun lesson.

**Reject.** Five *goals* as five planets. A post-run postcard that diagrams the campaign (that is a wiki). Difficulty select. HUD arrows (Miyamoto regretted showing the Star spawn; same trap).

## The Well — always first

**Attitude.** Hold the wet lip. Mercy needs ten minutes. This is the job. First OutRun stage: do not punish existing.

**Clock.**

| Time | Beat |
|------|------|
| 0:00 | Launch. Hatch coaches. **No Vicar VO** (WORLD.md). LEVEL1.md still says "Vicar / Hatch comms" — Chair call already flagged in CLASSICS / WRITERS_ROOM; do not silently patch. |
| early | Horde scales. Cardinal caches: patch, scoop, flare. Pip names gadgets, then the cost. |
| ~8:00 | Mercy jump. Guardian (alarm) spawns. Arms, then heart. |
| 10:00 | Mercy leaves if you held. Default road = A. |

**Fly through.** Open wet mouth. Nest wood-and-brass vs Banner cheap red coming in as curtain (procedure, not a speech). All-range — you can leave the tunnel because there is no tunnel (SF2 lesson, already stolen). Caches are geometry at cardinals. Off-cardinal buoy is the Dry Mouth star, readable, not a one-pixel gap. Gold cache glow is Kite's billboard, not a lore chest.

**Radio.** Hatch: rolls, leaks, the monkey, your name if the hull folds. Gets quiet at 2 hull, not cute. Pip: caches, hot guns, hit the chest. Kite: 1–2 times *if* he hears you — name as compliment and threat, enjoys the light show, no manifesto. No fourth voice. No "what is the Banner?"

**How it forks (visible first, radio backup).**

OutRun checkpoint is the last two minutes, not a menu at 10:01.

1. **Easy road (A).** Survive. Hatch "Mercy's clear. Get out." Hangar. No cine. Fake bow. Miss a medal, you are here. That is Katina.
2. **Stay / buoy (C).** After she jumps, the mouth is still there and empty — you can *see* empty. 30s greedy flight can warp Dry Mouth *now*. Off-cardinal buoy scoops the same offer for next launch (or encore same night). Hatch names it when you take it, not before.
3. **Wrong window (Paint, not true).** Kite hears you: take a hit with 1 hull and live, or sit in a gold cache too long. Gold lip should be *visible* as paint, not as a stage name. Kite: "Wrong window, Rook." Then shut up. Two keys is already a Chair call in CLASSICS — this file does not cut them.
4. **Hard road (Wake).** Medal = never drop hull (or never spend Q) — one verb, not a score. Hop-pack: hop 8+ in one go; the pack is a thing you can see, not a style meter. With medal, the peel is weather in the sky. Pip "Wake's open." Then shut up.

Do not: HUD arrow to the peel. Pause unlock. Route recap. Combo meter for hops. Vicar briefing. "True path" copy.
