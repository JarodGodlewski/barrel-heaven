## Encore rules (no Mercy clock)

Mercy clock = hold the wet lip until the seed-ship jumps. Guardian ~8:00–10:00. That happens **once**, on the Well.

Wake and Paint are not that:

1. **No second Guardian-at-8:00.** The monkey on the Well is the alarm. The Lock is inside it later. Wake/Paint do not grow a mid-encore boss-hold.
2. **No second seed-ship jump.** Mercy peels the wet lip at Well 10:00 and is gone. WORLD: on B she is already on the other side.
3. **Clock is OutRun checkpoint, not VS Reaper.** Encore timer is a *cap you must reach the fork inside*. Stall = miss the window. Survive-to-Reaper would make Wake a second Well.
4. **No hangar, no kit select, no chits shop between jobs.** One sitting. Unlock ≠ skip (already PROGRESSION).
5. **Same hole.** Wake = the peel (wet lip emptying). Paint = gold lip. Lock = inside the monkey. If Paint plays as a church in the sky, CLASSICS already flagged the one-hole break.
6. **Existing radio only.** Pip "Wake's open." / Kite "Wrong window, Rook." / Hatch quiet on the Lock. No new voices. No "this is the encore" toast. No "true ending unlocked."
7. **Miss a link, fall back to A.** Medal dies, you do not ride the Wake. That is Star Fox, not a retry menu.

VS Inverse / Hurry stay off this string. Inverse curtain is a Well modifier after Dry Mouth + one Yolk (PROGRESSION beat 5). Hurry is a VS convenience we do not need; B is supposed to be long enough to be the movie and short enough to be one night.
