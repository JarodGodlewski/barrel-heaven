**Nest-clothes.** Medal lights Scrap Wake. Pip's one line, already locked: "Wake's open." Then shut up. Do not narrate "you medal'd." Don't chase Mercy on the peel — greedy stay is Dry Mouth, not Wake.

**Unlock / execute.** First clean Well stamps Wake forever (Isaac). Ending B still wants a clean Well *tonight* (Star Fox). Farming Wake from the hangar later is a mark, not the movie.
